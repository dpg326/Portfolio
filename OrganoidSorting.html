Organoid Sorting Via Image Machine Learning

An exploration into the potential of Machine Learning in cancer diagnostics

Project Overview

Organoid Sorting via Image Machine Learning was a summer-long project I undertook under the support of Lehigh Creative Inquiry. It was a interdisciplinary project with the bioengineering labratory. Personally, I oversaw training various models using both PyTorch and Tensorflow, performed data hygeine on brightfield Images, and developed frontend user interfaces for image classification. At the program's closure, we got to present our research at Lehigh's Summer Expo, in which we won first prize in research. Due to lab confidentiality, I can't disclose too many details on my work.
