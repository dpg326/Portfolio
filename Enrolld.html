Enrolld

CSE Enrollment Forecaster

A comprehensive course planning and enrollment forecasting system for Lehigh University CSE students

📅 November 2025 • 👥 Team of 4 • 🎓 Lehigh University

Project Overview

Registration at universities is often an overly complicated process that causes significant issues for students, advisors, and course coordinators. Coordinators struggle to accurately predict demand for elective courses, leading to classes being either overfilled or inaccessible. Communication between advisors and students is fragmented across multiple tools, and many students are unable to take required courses at the right time, delaying their degree progression.

The CSE Enrollment Forecaster (Enrolld) addresses these challenges by providing a unified platform that improves and supports the registration process for all stakeholders. The system allows students to plan their schedules ahead of registration, provides coordinators with valuable enrollment data for better course scheduling, and streamlines advisor-student communication.

As the fourth-year development team, we focused on integrating the interactive course flowchart into the main application, enhancing the user experience based on UI/UX feedback, and developing new features to support the student planning process. Our work built upon three years of prior development, taking the project from a minimum viable product to a fully-featured enrollment forecasting system.

Project Evolution

Year 1 (Spring 2022)

Established the foundation with a minimum viable product. Students could indicate course interest through planning tools, and coordinators received basic enrollment data.

Year 2

Focused on implementing flowchart functionality and restructuring the database for better scalability and performance.

Year 3

Emphasized feature-driven development and solidified individual user personas (students, advisors, coordinators). Added course flowchart visualization, though it remained on a separate branch.

Year 4 (Our Team) - November 2025

Integrated the flowchart into the main application, making it a core feature rather than a separate component. Enhanced user experience through UI/UX improvements and smart feature integration.

Key focus: Integration, polish, and user experience optimization
