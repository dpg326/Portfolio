Octo- CSE216 Semester Project.

A secure internal social media site.

Project Overview

Octo was a semester-long CSE216 Software Engineering project I worked on with 4 colleagues. To practice a breadth of Software Engineering disciplines, we rotated roles every 3 weeks, continuing on the prior member's work. These roles included Mobile, Web Frontend, Backend, Admin, and Project Manager. We were given a simple problem statement of a company needing an interna social media site for its employees to preserve company secrecy. I learned many valuable lessons from this project from Agile methodology to team work and collaboration.
